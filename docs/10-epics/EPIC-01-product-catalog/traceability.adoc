[NOTE]
====
*How to read this matrix* +
The *Status* column reflects the last CI run. icon:check-circle[role=green] means all
scenarios for that story passed. icon:times-circle[role=red] means at least one failed
and the build was blocked. icon:question-circle[role=gray] means no test results were
found (run `mvn verify -Pdocs` to generate them).
